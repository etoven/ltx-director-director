# Movie soundtrack samples

Open **Sound samples** in `../index.html` to preview and download the 17 WAV files. Search by scene/body part or filter by film. Samples also appear beside linked effects and on footage cards. Playback is manual.

`source/` contains audio-only PCM extracts from the existing short movie/trailer clips in `reference_clips/`. They retain the original mixed soundtrack and may contain dialogue, music and overlapping effects. They are not clean foley stems. Source rights remain with the respective owners.

`../sources/audio.json` records source provenance, duration, format, levels and effect associations. Associations identify the linked scene; they do not establish an isolated sound for the body change. `../sources/audio_index.md` lists every WAV.

All samples are stereo, 48 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV. Sound recipes FX073–FX088 remain written instructions; separate recordings for those recipes are not supplied. Previewing does not upload, attach or apply the sound to a model.

When importing over an older edition, remove its standalone demo recordings and obsolete audio mappings from the imported project. Preserve existing user-owned recordings. Use recorded foley or actual source excerpts for future sample additions rather than procedural or keyboard-like synthesized replacements.
