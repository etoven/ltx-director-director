# Aurora a111: useful Effects media tiles and thumbnail maintenance

The 17 source soundtrack tiles now have authored sound-design refinement instructions instead of empty prompts. The 17 reference clips also appear as editable video tiles with direction drawn from their catalog evidence and transfer scope. All 134 bundled entries have prompts and retain Show in menu off by default. Nonempty user overrides remain authoritative; the old inherited empty soundtrack prompt is filled without changing the catalog JSON. Applying a source tile refines text and never attaches its recording or clip automatically.

The redundant still/video/audio selector and separate Play button have been removed. The selected video tile autoplays silently; a recording plays or stops when clicked. Alternate previews and original media exports remain in the tile context menu, with the selected sample's Save action still available. Audio thumbnails retain their centered play glyph. The supplied artwork is unchanged. Catalog clip metadata's descriptive audio string is kept as provenance, not mistaken for a playable audio filename.

Seventeen silent software-encoded previews fit 320 × 200 at 12 fps and total 357,408 bytes. Original excerpts and WAV files remain intact. A cancellable background job runs at startup and when custom media changes. It checks supplied images and hashes of validated preview derivatives, regenerates damaged caches, and repairs missing/broken posters from video frames or a sound waveform. Custom preview encoding is bounded to eight seconds. Cache files are written atomically under Qt's application cache root; originals, settings and model runtimes are untouched. Stale or closed-window callbacks are discarded, and completed checks do not interrupt project playback or a sound already playing.

Validation: all six library/software-media checks and three isolated native UI groups passed. Tests decode every shipped derivative, assert dimensions/duration/no audio, verify source checksums, regenerate deliberately corrupt video/poster caches, exercise cancellation/missing sources, and cover prompt migration, overrides, playback interaction, drag acceptance and stale results. A complete startup audit checked 136 tiles including migrated defaults, with zero broken assets. Native library and soundtrack captures update the illustrated/offline help. No model inference or GPU test was run. Installed-wheel and release CI verification complete delivery.

The private style guide records tile playback, source-art preservation, context-menu access and thumbnail repair behavior.

The installed a111 wheel passed the complete catalog audit with 134 prompted bundled tiles, source/asset integrity checks and all 17 compressed previews present. Existing Flux canvas, native video request, seed/reference wiring, LoRA persistence, onboarding and Downloads anchoring checks also passed. The final native preview check covers click-to-stop and verifies that export copies the full original clip rather than its thumbnail derivative. Original catalog JSON, artwork, excerpts and sound files have no changes.
