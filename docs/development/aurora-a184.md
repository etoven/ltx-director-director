# Aurora a184: full-reference video extension defaults

New video extension groups, including Insert video for blend and V+, default to MiniMax Full (Ref2VA). Existing groups keep their authored workspace. The bridge remains a text-only segment with the standard incoming/outgoing conditional frame boundaries; no reference-panel media is synthesized. The inserted movie remains a full-length group attachment.

The single focused insertion workflow verifies the new groups use Full while the preexisting group stays Standard, preserving bridge timing, boundary context and attached media. No model renders or broad suite.
